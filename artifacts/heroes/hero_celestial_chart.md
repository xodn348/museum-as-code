# Stone Constellation Chart (Cheonsang Yeolcha Bunyajido)

## Why this matters

It changes the emotional register of the set: from sacred faces and royal gold to science, measurement, and the night sky.

## What you're looking at

The chart organizes stars inside circles and explanatory inscriptions. Rather than illustrating a mythic heavens, it presents observation, order, and calendrical authority as statecraft.

## The story

Created soon after the founding of Joseon, Cheonsang Yeolcha Bunyajido linked astronomy with royal legitimacy. It also preserves older astronomical traditions, turning inherited sky knowledge into a new dynasty’s monument.
