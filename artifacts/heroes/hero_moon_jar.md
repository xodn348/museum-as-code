# White Porcelain Moon Jar

## Why this matters

Foreign visitors often recognize moon jars as the Korean answer to minimalism: generous, handmade, and never mechanically perfect.

## What you're looking at

Large moon jars were usually formed from two hemispheres joined at the center, which helps explain their quiet irregularity. The surface is not blank; it is a field of milky glaze, soft shadow, and small deviations that make the object feel alive.

## The story

Made in Joseon kilns connected with court supply, moon jars later became a modern design icon. Their power lies in refusing spectacle: after crowns and incense burners, the moon jar asks the page to slow down and look at white space.
