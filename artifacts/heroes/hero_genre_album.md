# Album of Genre Paintings by Kim Hong-do (Danwon)

## Why this matters

The set needs people. Danwon’s album makes Korean heritage feel inhabited, funny, physical, and social.

## What you're looking at

The paintings often leave the background sparse and concentrate on bodies in action. In Wrestling, spectators form a ring around two athletes, and a vendor and scattered shoes complete the composition.

## The story

Kim Hong-do’s genre paintings are valued for their warm but sharp observation of common life in late Joseon. Treated as one hero slot, the album can open into several leaves rather than reducing the work to a single famous image.
