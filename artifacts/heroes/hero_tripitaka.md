# Printing Woodblocks of the Tripiṭaka Koreana

## Why this matters

For a code museum, the Tripiṭaka Koreana feels like an early data infrastructure: text, format, duplication, storage, and long-term preservation.

## What you're looking at

Each block carries carved characters in reverse for printing. The scale matters as much as the individual craft: tens of thousands of blocks had to remain consistent enough to transmit a complete canon.

## The story

Carved during the Goryeo period, the woodblocks are kept at Haeinsa. They were made under conditions of crisis and devotion, turning religious text into a durable national archive.
