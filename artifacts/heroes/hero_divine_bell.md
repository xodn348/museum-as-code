# Divine Bell of King Seongdeok

## Why this matters

The bell expands the experience beyond looking. It brings scale, inscription, Buddhist imagery, and the memory of sound into the digital museum.

## What you're looking at

Dragon-shaped ornaments form the suspension loop, while a reverberation pipe rises near the crown. Decorative bands, lotus panels, and apsaras holding incense burners organize the enormous bronze surface.

## The story

Completed in 771, the bell is also known as the Bell of Bongdeoksa Temple and, popularly, the Emille Bell. Its legend is famous, but its technical achievement is just as powerful: casting, relief, and acoustic design on a monumental scale.
