# Gold Crown from Geumgwanchong Tomb

## Why this matters

Gold needs no translation. This crown gives the sequence a flash of Silla spectacle and an immediately readable image of ancient kingship.

## What you're looking at

The crown is built from a headband with vertical branch-shaped uprights and suspended ornaments. Thin gold sheet, stamped patterns, and curved jade pieces make it shimmer with movement even when still.

## The story

Silla tombs preserved crowns, belts, vessels, and horse gear as part of an elite burial world. The Geumgwanchong crown remains one of the clearest images of that world: a ruler framed by trees, antlers, light, and ritual authority.
