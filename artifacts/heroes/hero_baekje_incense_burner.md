# Great Gilt-bronze Incense Burner of Baekje

## Why this matters

No other object in the set carries so many worlds at once: scent, ritual, animals, music, immortals, mountains, and smoke.

## What you're looking at

The burner rises from a dragon base to a lotus bowl and a mountain-shaped lid topped by a phoenix. Small openings among the peaks let incense smoke drift through the landscape, animating the metal with air.

## The story

Discovered at Neungsan-ri in Buyeo, the burner shows Baekje’s ability to absorb Buddhist and Daoist imagery into a distinct court style. It is ideal for a scroll page because every level rewards close looking.
