# Celadon Prunus Vase with Inlaid Cloud and Crane Design

## Why this matters

Goryeo celadon is one of Korea’s most internationally recognizable arts, and this form offers a perfect visual pause between bronze, gold, paper, and stone.

## What you're looking at

A maebyeong narrows at the mouth, expands powerfully at the shoulder, and tightens again near the foot. The inlay technique cuts designs into the clay and fills them with contrasting slip before glazing, allowing cranes and clouds to hover inside the blue-green surface rather than sit on top of it.

## The story

Celadon reached a technical and poetic peak in Goryeo. Cranes suggest longevity and ascent, while the glaze evokes water, sky, and polished jade. Even where the exact hero image still needs replacement, the page documents the public-source constraint rather than pretending certainty.
