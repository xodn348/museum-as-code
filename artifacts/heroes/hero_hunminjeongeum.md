# Hunminjeongeum Manuscript (Haerye Edition)

## Why this matters

For Museum as Code, this is the conceptual anchor: a language object that explains its own design decisions.

## What you're looking at

The Haerye edition contains King Sejong’s preface and examples, followed by scholarly explanations of the principles behind the letters. It is not merely a display of beautiful script; it is a technical document about phonology, access, and design.

## The story

Published in 1446, Hunminjeongeum made a new alphabet public and explained it with unusual clarity. In this project, the `.hgl` panel is not decoration beside the manuscript; it is a contemporary echo of Korea’s long habit of treating language as infrastructure.
